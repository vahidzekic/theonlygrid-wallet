Privacy policy

Applies to the TOG Wallet app (Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android). Last updated: 19 November 2026.

1. Controller

The controller of personal data is Vahid Zekić, Zmaj Jovina 38, 36300 Novi Pazar, Serbia, vahidzekic@gmail.com ("we").

2. Summary

  • Only you hold your signing keys. We cannot move your TOG.
  • The account is your TOG account, the same as for TOG Drive; account data is processed as described in the TOG Drive privacy policy, section 3.1.
  • Transactions on The Only Grid are public and permanent, as on any public blockchain.
  • No ads, tracking, analytics or profiling.

3. Data we process

3.1 TOG account

Username, login record (OPAQUE, from which the password cannot be learned), locked keys, public keys and the device list. The same data as for TOG Drive, kept by the TOG account service.

3.2 Network requests

When the app reads balances and history or sends a transaction, the public RPC gateway (rpc.theonlygrid.com) sees your IP address, the time, the address you read and the transaction you send. Server logs keep the IP address, time, request type and outcome.

3.3 Faucet (testnet)

When you ask for test-TOG, the faucet processes your address and IP address (for the once-per-24-hours limit), and Cloudflare Turnstile runs a check in your browser.

3.4 Data on your device

The app keeps the device keys and the locked root key in the system keystore, a session token, settings and the addresses you watch. They don't leave the device.

3.5 Support

If you write to us, we process your email address and message to answer.

4. What is public on the network

Every transaction on The Only Grid is public: sender address, recipient address, amount and time. Anyone can see them in the network explorer, and they cannot be deleted. An address alone does not contain your name, but once you link it to yourself (for example by publishing it), anyone can see its transactions.

5. What we don't collect

  • your password, your 24 words or your signing keys;
  • email, phone number, name, home address or payment data;
  • location, contacts, photos; the camera is used only to scan QR codes and the image never leaves the device;
  • advertising identifiers or tracking data.

6. Purposes and legal basis

  • Providing the service (account, reading balances, sending transactions): performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Security and abuse prevention (logs, faucet limits): legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Legal obligations: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

7. Recipients

  • Contabo GmbH, Germany, server provider for the gateway, faucet and account service, as processor.
  • Cloudflare, Inc., for the faucet and sign-up checks.
  • Operators of The Only Grid nodes, who receive and store public transactions.
  • Apple and Google, when you get the app from their stores.
  • Public authorities, only where the law requires.

8. Retention

  • TOG account: as long as it exists.
  • Server logs with IP addresses: 14 days, unless needed for a security incident or by law.
  • Faucet records (address, IP): for the limit period, at most 30 days.
  • Transactions on the network: permanently, as part of the public chain.

9. Account deletion

You can delete your TOG account at any time, free of charge: in the app under Settings → Delete account, or by writing to vahidzekic@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion" and your username.

  • Move your TOG to another address first. After deletion the key for your address no longer exists, so any TOG left there can never be moved.
  • Deleting the TOG account also deletes your TOG Drive, as it is the same account.
  • Username, login record, locked keys, devices and sessions are deleted at once; server logs within 30 days.
  • Transactions on the network cannot be deleted (section 4).

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Serbian Personal Data Protection Act you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to the Serbian Commissioner (www.poverenik.rs) or a supervisory authority in the EU. Send requests to vahidzekic@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

11. Children

TOG Wallet is not meant for children under 16.

12. Changes

A new version will be published on this page with a new date; we will tell you about material changes in the app.
