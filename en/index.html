TOG Wallet is the official wallet for $TOG, the native coin of The Only Grid. You sign in with the same TOG account you use for TOG Drive, and the key that signs your transactions is made and kept on your device.

What you get

One TOG account

The same account and the same 24 words as TOG Drive and the TOG Console. The same address everywhere.

No transfer fee

A $TOG transfer on the Hub carries no fee for now. Before you confirm you see the recipient, the amount and what remains.

Receive with a QR code

Your address as text and as a QR code, with an optional amount, easy to share.

History

Every payment in and out of your account, with the block height and a link to the network explorer.

Test-TOG in one tap

On the testnet, the Get test-TOG button fills your wallet from the public faucet.

Your key stays with you

The app builds and signs each transaction on your device. The server only relays it and cannot move your TOG.

App lock

Fingerprint, Face ID, Windows Hello or your system password before every send and before showing the phrase.

Every device

Windows, macOS, Linux, iPhone and Android, with the same account.

Getting started

  1. Download the app for your system from the releases page.
  2. Sign in with your TOG account. If you use TOG Drive, it is the same account. If you have none, create one in the app.
  3. Write down the 24 words if the account is new. They restore both the wallet and Drive.
  4. Get test-TOG with the button on the home screen and send your first transaction.

Details are in the guide; what happens when you send TOG is in how it works.

What we don't do

  • We don't hold your keys and can't move your TOG.
  • No ads, tracking or analytics in the app.
  • We don't sell or rent data.
  • We don't ask for an email or phone number for an account.

What is public on the network anyway, and what the server sees, is in the privacy policy.
