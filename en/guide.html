Guide

For TOG Wallet on The Only Grid testnet.

Installing

Windows

Download the .zip from the releases page, unpack it and run tog_wallet.exe. The app is not signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.

macOS

Download the macOS .zip, unpack it and drag TOG Wallet to Applications. Until the app is notarized, open it the first time with right-click → Open.

Linux

Download the .tar.gz, unpack it and run tog_wallet. Keys are stored with the Secret Service (GNOME Keyring or KWallet).

iPhone and Android

The mobile versions go to testers first (TestFlight and Google Play internal testing). We will announce here when they are public.

Signing in

TOG Wallet uses your TOG account, the same as TOG Drive. You sign in with your username and password. The password never leaves the device in readable form: login uses the OPAQUE protocol.

Without an account, choose Create account. While open registration is closed you need an invite code or a browser check. The app then creates your keys and a 24-word phrase.

Your network address is derived from the account's root key, so it is the same on every device and in the TOG Console.

The 24 words

The phrase is the backup of your keys. Write it on paper and keep it safe; never give it to anyone or type it into a web page. TOG never asks for it except on the recovery screen in the app.

The phrase, together with the TOG account service, restores your account, wallet and Drive. Without the password and the phrase the account cannot be restored, not even by us.

Receiving

The Receive screen shows your address (0x and 40 hex digits) and a QR code. Give the sender the address or let them scan the code. A payment shows as soon as it is in a block, usually within seconds.

Sending

  1. On Send, enter or scan the recipient's address and the amount.
  2. Check the whole confirmation screen: recipient, amount, what remains, network.
  3. Confirm with your fingerprint, face or system password.

Network rules: the sender must hold at least 0.1 TOG before sending, and the first payment to an address that does not exist yet must be at least 0.1 TOG. A Hub transfer has no fee for now.

Always check the whole address, not just its start and end: scammers make addresses that differ only in the middle.

Test-TOG

On the testnet, Get test-TOG opens a short browser check, then the faucet sends test-TOG to your address. Each address and each IP address can ask once every 24 hours. Test-TOG has no monetary value.

App lock

The app asks for your device unlock before every send and before showing the phrase. You can also turn on a lock at start in Settings.

FAQ

Is this the same account as TOG Drive?

Yes. One TOG account, one phrase, one address.

Can anyone at TOG move my TOG?

No. The signing key exists only on your devices. The service keeps only a locked copy of the root key, which it cannot open.

I forgot my password.

Choose Recover with phrase, enter the 24 words and set a new password.

I lost my phone.

Sign in on another device and revoke the lost one in the device list. Your TOG is on the network, not on the phone.

A transaction stays "not confirmed".

Retry it from the history. A retry reuses the same transaction number, so it can never pay twice.
