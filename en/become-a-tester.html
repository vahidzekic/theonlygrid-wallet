Become a tester

A closed test of the TOG Wallet Android app on Google Play.

What it is

Before its public release on Google Play, TOG Wallet for Android goes through a closed test. We are looking for at least 12 people to install the app and use it for 14 days, so we can find bugs before it reaches everyone.

Testnet only. The app runs on The Only Grid testnet, with test-TOG that has no monetary value. You don't buy anything and you can't lose anything.

Who can join

  • Anyone with an Android phone and a Gmail (Google) account.
  • You need to stay opted in to the test for at least 14 days and open the app now and then.

How to join

  1. Send your Gmail address to vahidzekic@gmail.com, for example with the subject "TOG Wallet tester".
  2. We add you to the tester list on Google Play.
  3. You get an opt-in link for the test. Open it on your phone and accept the invitation.
  4. Install TOG Wallet from Google Play, sign in with your TOG account or create one in the app, and get test-TOG.

Report a bug or an idea on GitHub or at vahidzekic@gmail.com.

iPhone

For now the TestFlight test is internal only: Apple requires an organization account for wallet apps. iPhone testing is coming later.

FAQ

Is it safe?

Yes. TOG Wallet is non-custodial: the keys are made and kept on your device, and nobody else can move your TOG. The test runs only on the testnet, with test-TOG that has no value. What is processed is in the privacy policy.

Can I leave?

Yes, at any time, through the same opt-in link or on Google Play. But please stay for at least 14 days, because Google requires a test that long before a public release.

TOG Wallet is a test version on The Only Grid testnet. Test-TOG has no monetary value.
